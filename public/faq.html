Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about trading skills, earning SkillCredits, and getting help with design, writing, coding, and technical projects on SkillSwap.

Where can I find someone to design something if I offer my skills in return?

SkillSwap is a peer-to-peer platform where you can find designers for UI/UX, graphic design, logos, and branding by offering your own skills in return. Using SkillCredits, you can trade coding, writing, video editing, translation, or tech help for professional design work without using money. You can explore open design swaps or create your own swap offer today.

Where can I find a skill-swap community?

SkillSwap provides a dedicated online skill-swap community where creators, freelancers, and learners exchange talents. On SkillSwap, you can browse open skill swaps across categories like design, development, writing, and video editing, connect with collaborators, and earn or spend SkillCredits in a fair, secure ecosystem. Sign up for free to join our growing community.

Can I trade writing skills for logo/design help?

Yes! On SkillSwap, you can directly trade writing skills—such as copywriting, technical writing, or content creation—for logo design and graphic assets. You can post a custom swap offer describing what you need and what you offer, or request an existing design swap using your SkillCredits. Check out available design listings on our Explore Swaps marketplace.

Where can I trade my skills for someone else's skills?

You can trade your skills for someone else's skills on SkillSwap. The platform features an open marketplace where members offer and request services in design, web development, content writing, video production, language learning, and technical support using SkillCredits. Simply list the skill you offer and the service you need in return.

Where can I get free tech help by offering my own skills in return?

SkillSwap allows you to get free technical support, software troubleshooting, website debugging, or coding assistance by offering your own skills in return. You earn SkillCredits by helping others with your talents and spend those credits to receive tech help from experienced community members. Start by browsing tech help swaps or offering your expertise on SkillSwap.
